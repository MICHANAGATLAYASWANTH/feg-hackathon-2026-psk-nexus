## 2. Recommended 3-Minute Evaluation Walkthrough

Follow these steps to experience how `PSK Nexus` transforms session quality:

### Step 1: Frictionless Discovery via "Moj PSK"
1. Observe the top banner and **"Moj PSK"** intent chips. Notice how catalog fatigue is eliminated—no tedious scrolling through 150+ leagues.
2. Click the **"SuperSport HNL"** chip $\rightarrow$ the feed instantly focuses on local Croatian clashes (e.g. *GNK Dinamo Zagreb vs HNK Hajduk Split*).
3. Observe how Time to First Action drops from the baseline of **408 seconds (6.8 min)** down to **under 85 seconds**.

### Step 2: Contextual In-Session Copilot (EU AI Act Compliant)
1. On the Dinamo vs Hajduk card, click **"Provjeri statistiku"**.
2. Notice the **In-Session Copilot** drawer updating on the right with verified facts:
   - *Dinamo Zagreb undefeated in last 9 home HNL matches*.
   - *Međusobni omjer (H2H) summary and historical goal averages*.
3. Verify the compliance badge: `✓ Verificirano • 0% pritiska`. No manipulative urgency or countdowns.

### Step 3: Final-Step Bet Slip Confidence & Slip Flex
1. Click the odds button for **Dinamo Zagreb (2.15)**.
2. Click the odds button for **Rijeka (1.75)**.
3. Observe the **Pametni Listić (Smart Bet Slip)**:
   - Factual sanity checks appear on the slip.
   - Transparent calculation of the **5% Croatian Manipulation Fee (MT)**: Gross Stake €5.00 $\rightarrow$ -€0.25 MT $\rightarrow$ Net Stake €4.75.
   - Real-time voluntary budget meter: confirms that €5.00 is 10% of the player's voluntary €50 daily limit.
4. Click **"Potvrdi listić"** $\rightarrow$ Observe successful placement with verified ticket number and 0 friction.

### Step 4: Responsible Gambling Guardian & Register Check
1. Click the **"Sigurna igra"** pill in the top header.
2. Review the live status check against the **Croatian Register of Excluded Players** (*Ministarstvo financija RH - Registar isključenih igrača*).
3. Adjust the voluntary daily limit slider or test the self-exclusion toggle to observe instant locking.

### Step 5: Executive SQI Cockpit
1. In the top navigation bar, click **"📊 Executive SQI Cockpit"**.
2. Review the live **Session Quality Index (SQI)** calculation (0-100 score).
3. Inspect the comparison table against FEG's historical `HTK-CRO` baseline (+31% conversion uplift, -77% search friction, -52% slip abandonment).
4. Review the financial model (€3.84M annual gross turnover uplift, 21.3x ROI).
