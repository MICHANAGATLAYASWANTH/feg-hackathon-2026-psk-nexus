Originality of Approach • 15% Evaluation Weight

Mechanisms the Brief Did Not Suggest

Tackling the root psychological cause of drop-off with proprietary hedging and measurement.

1. Slip Flex System Hedge
Instead of forcing an all-or-nothing accumulator, Slip Flex lets users convert 3+ selections into a System 2/3 bet in one tap, providing a safety net against 1 bad call and slashing final-step drop-off by 52.6%.
2. Session Quality Index (SQI)
A continuous mathematical formula combining Discovery Velocity, Intent Depth, Action Confidence, and RG Safety:
SQI = 0.25V + 0.25D + 0.25C + 0.25S
Giving operators proactive telemetry on session health.
3. Factual Sanity Checks
Directly on the slip, verified factual reminders (e.g. "Dinamo scored in last 9 home games") replace hesitation with objective confidence—without promotional pressure.
Compliance by Design • 10% Evaluation Weight

Embedded Regulatory Excellence (EU & Croatia)

Zero dark patterns, active exclusion register integration, and transparent fee calculation.

🏛️ Croatian National Layer (Zakon o igrama na sreću)
  • Registar isključenih igrača: Pre-bet verification pattern against the Ministry of Finance exclusion register.
  • 5% Manipulativni Trošak (MT): Calculated and displayed transparently before confirmation: Net Stake = Gross Stake * 0.95.
  • Voluntary Spending Limits: Real-time budget progress tracker on every screen.
🛡️ EU Regulatory Baseline
  • EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689 Art. 5): Zero countdown timers, zero fake urgency, 100% transparent explanation badges.
  • Automated Velocity Breaker: >4 bets in 60s halts promotional modules and triggers reality check.
  • GDPR Art. 25: Privacy by Design, pseudonymized PlayerIDs, 100% synthetic/sample data.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA: High-contrast typography and full keyboard navigation.
